# Roadmap

- **v0.1.0 — local MVP:** assets and borrowers, list/filter, transactions for borrow/return, OpenAPI description, local demo, tests and CI.
- **Next:** authentication and role-based authorization, threat review, audit trail, migration strategy, pagination, and integration tests. A public writable demonstration must wait until authentication and authorization are designed and verified.
- **Later:** safer deployment example, lending policies such as due dates, and an opt-in pilot with a lab or campus organization. Record feedback only after it actually exists.

These are planned milestones, not claims of implementation, deployment, security certification, or adoption.
